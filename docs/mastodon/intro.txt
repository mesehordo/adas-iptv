Meet Adás 📺

A free, open-source player for public live TV channels, radio stations and podcasts, with a streaming-style interface.

• TV guide, favourites, profiles (with a kids profile)
• Radio and podcasts with subscriptions
• Many styles and layouts, also for the sofa with a remote or a controller

For Windows, macOS, Linux and Android (TV too). Adás provides no content itself: it only plays public streams.

https://mesehordo.github.io/adas-iptv/
